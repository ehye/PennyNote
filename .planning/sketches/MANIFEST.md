# Sketch Manifest

## Design Direction
Selected warm notebook with system sans-serif typography, tighter corners, minimal copy, and 16px mobile side spacing. Home shows category/icon-based recent notes grouped by Day/Week/Month and a New note button. Mobile Review shows a current-month expense pie and grouped totals; desktop Overview keeps advanced charts and filters. Accounts use type/account nesting with balances; Settings configures two-level categories and icons. Currencies follow accounts and are never combined; transfers stay out of expense/income totals.
